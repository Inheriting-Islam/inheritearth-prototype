## License notes

- CC BY / CC BY-SA images require visible attribution (author + license, ideally linked) wherever used — e.g. a photo-credits section or footer on the site.
- CC BY-SA additionally requires that any *derivative of the image itself* be shared under the same license (resizing/cropping for web use is fine with attribution).
- CC0 images (kl-skyline.jpg, lombok.jpg) need no attribution, but crediting Walkerssk and Lasthib is good practice.
- Privacy check: no identifiable children's faces in any image. family.jpg shows people (including children) only as full silhouettes against sunset; kl-street.jpg is an ambient adult crowd.
