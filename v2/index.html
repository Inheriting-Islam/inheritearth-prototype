Visas & residency

Three real routes for families — and what each is for.

MM2H (Silver) Long-haul families

  • Fixed deposit: US$150,000
  • Pass length: 5 years, renewable
  • Kids can study: Yes
  • Spouse can work: No (separate permit)
  • Path to PR: Discretionary, slow
✓ Verify · official MM2H portal ↗

DE Rantau Remote workers

  • Income floor: ~US$24,000/yr (remote)
  • Pass length: 12 months, renewable once
  • Dependents: Yes
  • Kids can study: Yes (int'l schools)
  • Path to PR: None — a trial run, not a home
✓ Verify · MDEC DE Rantau ↗

Employment Pass If you're hired

  • Requires: Malaysian employer sponsor
  • Dependents: Yes, with schooling
  • Spouse work: Possible via own permit
  • Stability: Tied to the job
✓ Verify · ESD Malaysia ↗
The permanence question, answered straight: Malaysia will let your family live well for decades — but permanent residency is discretionary and citizenship is rare. If "we need somewhere we can grow old with certainty" is your deal-breaker, weight residency high and read your results accordingly. We will never blur this.
Healthcare

The region's safety net — in English.

What we verified

Gleneagles KL, Prince Court, and Pantai carry JCI accreditation with 24/7 pediatric emergency, NICU capability, and English-speaking staff. Routine pediatric visits run $15–40; a family regional insurance plan $200–300/month. Medical tourism volume keeps standards market-tested.

What to weigh

Public hospitals are competent but crowded and Bahasa-first. Complex chronic care means the private system — insure accordingly. Haze weeks (usually Sep–Oct) matter for asthmatic kids; we track AQI seasonality in the live product.

Muslim life

Not accommodation — atmosphere.

The azan is ambient. Halal is the default at every table. Eid empties the city twice a year, schools plan around Ramadan, and every neighborhood keeps a surau within walking distance. For families coming from places where practicing takes planning, the change is the point.

The honest take — what the brochures skip

The heat is not a vibe, it's a constraint: outdoor life happens before 10am and after 5pm, and one family in our threads left after six months because of it. Traffic will shape where you live more than any neighborhood ranking — pick the school first, then live near it. Top-tier school fees rival Western private schools, and they rise every year. And you will likely always be a resident, not a citizen: Malaysia is generous with welcome and stingy with permanence.

If those four things are priced in, almost every family we've studied who chose KL stayed — or left for Penang.

Methodology & sources

The rules we score by.

A ranking you can't interrogate is a vibe with decimals. These are the operating rules behind every number on this site — they are the product.

01

Your weights do the ranking — we never editorialize the order.

Every match score is your 100-point allocation multiplied by city pillar scores, summed. Two families get two different rankings from identical data. The math is shown on every card under "Why this score?" — no hidden adjustments, no sponsored placement, ever.

02

Every fact carries a source and a date.

Visa rules cite the government portal. School fees cite the school's published schedule. Rents cite sampled listings. Each carries a "verified" date; when a fact ages past our freshness bar — 6 months for visas, 12 for fees and healthcare — it's flagged stale until re-checked by a human.

03

Thin data says so. Out loud.

Where our data is thin, the score is labeled "estimated," drawn with a hatched bar, and the city's confidence rating drops. We'd rather rank lower than invent certainty. Missing data is never silently scored as average-and-hidden.

04

Lived-experience data needs at least 10 independent reports before it becomes a number.

Community experience — Muslim life quality, Black-family belonging — is real data, but below 10 independent resident reports we show testimony with sourcing, never a score. Above the bar, the count and date range are displayed with the number.

05

Deal-breakers exclude cities publicly.

If a destination fails your hard constraints — no accredited international school, budget exceeded with school fees included — it's excluded with the exact reason stated, not quietly dropped. An excluded city with a great score is our honesty working as intended.

06

We publish information, not advice.

Nothing here is legal, immigration, tax, or financial advice, and match results are destinations to research — not visas you qualify for. For your specific case, always verify with official sources and licensed professionals. We link both, everywhere.

07

Prototype status, stated plainly.

This is a working prototype: five launch cities, scores built from desk research (Aug 2026), quotes drawn from cited public community threads, resident survey in progress. Anything not yet verified to the standard above is labeled — and the live product ships with the receipts visible on every number.
